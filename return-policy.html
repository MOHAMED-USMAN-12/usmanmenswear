7-Day Doorstep Return & Exchange Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Hassle-Free Sizing Guarantee
Complimentary Doorstep Exchange Wrong size or fit? We schedule a courier to pick up the item and deliver your replacement at zero additional shipping fee.
1. Eligibility Window

You may initiate an exchange or return request within 7 calendar days from the date of package delivery as recorded by our logistics carrier.

2. Condition Guidelines

Garments must be returned in their original, unwashed, unaltered condition with all original fabric care tags, branded collar stays, and presentation packaging intact.

3. Refund Processing Timelines

Once our atelier quality assurance team receives and verifies the returned garment (typically within 24–48 hours of pickup), refunds are released immediately:

  • UPI & Net Banking: 2 to 4 business hours directly to your source bank account.
  • Credit / Debit Cards: 3 to 5 business days depending on issuing banking partners.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Transferred directly to your designated bank account or UPI ID upon pickup confirmation.
4. How to Request an Exchange

Navigate to My Orders in your account dashboard, select the relevant order, and click "Initiate Return / Exchange", or email our concierge at concierge@usmanwear.com with your Order ID.
