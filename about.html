Mastery in Every Single Stitch

At Usman Mens Wear, we reject transient fast-fashion cycles. Every shirt in our collection begins in select long-staple cotton fields of the Nile Delta, is woven with two-ply Italian looms, and finished with precision 21-stitches-per-inch lockstitching.

From Savile Row-inspired double pleat trousers with side adjusters to Okayama shuttle-loomed selvedge denim, our silhouettes are designed to empower men with timeless presence and supreme daily comfort.
