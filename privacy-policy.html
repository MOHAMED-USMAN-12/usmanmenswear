Privacy & Data Security Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Usman Mens Wear Private Limited
1. Information We Collect

When you purchase garments from Usman Mens Wear, we collect the necessary customer data required to fulfill and deliver your luxury garments, including: your full name, shipping and billing address, email address, mobile number, and payment transaction identifiers provided through our payment partners.

2. Zero Raw Payment Card Storage

We do not store, process, or view raw debit/credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers. All transactions are securely tokenized through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway partners (including Razorpay, UPI NPCI rails, and major card networks).

3. Use of Personal Information

Your details are strictly utilized to: dispatch shipments via logistics partners (Bluedart, Delhivery), send shipment tracking notifications via SMS/email, manage size returns and refunds, and provide personalized customer care. We never sell, rent, or trade client information to third-party marketing brokers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes browser local storage and essential cookies to maintain your shopping bag items, saved wishlist items, and authenticated session preferences.

5. Contact Data Privacy Officer

For inquiries regarding data erasure or account deletion, contact our privacy compliance team at privacy@usmanwear.com.
